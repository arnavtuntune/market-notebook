## HDFC Bank (₹726, 39 shares)
- 1:1 bonus in Aug 2025, which is why the price looks low. Down ~30% from its Oct 2025 high.
- Q1 FY27: profit ₹19,060 cr (+5%), NII +7%, **NIM down to 3.26%** from 3.38%.
- Sep 2026 update: loans +16.3%, deposits +18.8%. Loan-to-deposit ratio ~97% vs ~110% right after the merger. CASA deposits growing only 10.8%, so funding is getting more expensive.
- Anup Bagchi (ex-ICICI) takes over as CEO on 27 Oct 2026.
- Trades at a ~26% price-to-book discount to ICICI Bank. Jefferies Buy, ₹880 target.
- **Risk:** margins keep slipping because deposits are expensive. Q2 results came out 17 Oct, one day after I bought, so check them.

## Maruti Suzuki (₹11,487, 2 shares)
- GST 2.0 (Sep 2025): small cars dropped from 28% + cess to 18%. Sep 2026 mini/compact sales +24%.
- **The RBI hiked rates by 25 bp to 5.50% on 7 Oct 2026**, so rate cuts no longer help.
- Market share 39.3% in FY26, a 13-year low. SUVs are ~67% of the market and Maruti is weak there.
- Q1 FY27 revenue +36% but **profit −11%**, margin ~8.4% vs ~12% (commodity costs).
- Record FY26 exports of 4.48 lakh units (+35%), 49% of India's car exports.
- Down ~33% in 2026.
- **Risk:** margins and market share keep falling even as volumes grow. This is a turnaround bet, not a quality bet.
